WorkTree redesign, first look

A garden, not a filing cabinet.

Plant shapes and flower colours on a clean, light base. This sheet shows the palette, three logo options, the restyled workflow nodes and three screens. Nothing here is final: tell me what to keep, change or drop, and I write the full guide to match.

1. Palette

Seven flower colours of equal strength instead of one green. The neutrals lean slightly violet, as on your earlier canvas board, so the colours stay bright and nothing turns muddy or brown.

Each area owns a colour. Home marigold, Operations hibiscus, Inbox orchid, Studio iris, Data lagoon, Connections morning glory, Insights fern. Each workflow owns a flower. Its colour and petal count follow it through lists, runs, charts and the canvas. Status keeps fixed meanings. Fern done, hibiscus failed, morning glory running, marigold waiting. Always with the word. Main button. Iris. Night soil for the strongest single action on a coloured band.

3. Workflow nodes, restyled

The plant idea from Rings and Bloom stays: shape says what a node does, colour says what family it belongs to. The thin tree rings are replaced by solid, simple flower forms that read at a glance and stay clear when small.

What I need from you

  1. Logo: A, B or C, or a mix (for example the petals of A with the trunk of B).
  2. Palette: are these the right colours and is this bright enough, or should it go further?
  3. Nodes: do solid flower shapes work, or do you want more detail and realism in the plants?
  4. Colour reach: is a coloured band per area plus coloured cards the right amount on the light base?
  5. Type: the rounded display face in the headings (Bricolage Grotesque) with Figtree for text. Keep, or stay with the system font?
